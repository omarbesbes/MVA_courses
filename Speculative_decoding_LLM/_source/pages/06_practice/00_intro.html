What you will be able to do after this page
  1. Explain why exact speculation gives little on image, speech and action tokens, and how a class of equivalent tokens replaces token identity there.
  2. Read a speed-up claim: list the conditions it depends on, and the extra measurements a lossy method owes.
  3. Say what “lossless” guarantees on real hardware, and what it does not.
  4. Name the lossy options that exist in serving software, and the parameters that are wrongly taken for lossy ones.
  5. List the documented risks of speculative decoding, exact and lossy.
  6. Choose a method for a given situation, and state the open problems.

0. Before you start

This page uses the vocabulary of the whole guide: the exact rule (page 1), the ceilings of exact methods (page 2), the relaxed rules and their trade-off (page 3), the judges (page 4) and the step-level methods (page 5). It can also be read alone by someone who must decide whether to switch a lossy option on: go to §5 and follow the links back.

Software changes quickly. The statements about serving engines describe their documentation as read in November 2026; version numbers are given so that you can check what has changed.

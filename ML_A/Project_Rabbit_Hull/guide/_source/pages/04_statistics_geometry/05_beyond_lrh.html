What the paper does and does not say

The paper writes that these patterns "are difficult to reconcile with a purely linear superposition model, and instead point toward additional geometric constraints" (§5 · p. 14). Its own limitations section (App. J · p. 51) says it refrains from stating that the results contradict the LRH. Each row above is compatible with a generalised linear model (non-negative codes, anisotropic covariance); the paper's step from the table to the MRH is the subject of page 5.

10. Summary and reflexes

What to remember
  • Occurrence (Fig. 9 A–C). Conditional energy against firing count has a triangular envelope; three dense positional concepts (left, right, bottom) fire on about $26$–$31\%$ of tokens (our estimate) and take about $11\%$ of all activations: a hybrid sparse + dense code.
  • Co-activation (Fig. 9 right). The spectrum of $\mZ\T\mZ$ is smooth, between a random and a shuffled baseline that differ by a factor of about $1.3$: weak evidence of no modules.
  • Geometry (Fig. 10). Welch $0.0356$; Grassmannian $0.0659$; random up to about $0.23$; the SAE up to about $0.9$. Singular values: tight-frame level $6.455$, MP band $[5.45,7.45]$, SAE from $22.5$ down to $0$: anisotropy. Hoyer $0.22$–$0.25$: distributed atoms.
  • Antipodal pairs (Fig. 11 A). A signed feature needs two opposite atoms when codes are non-negative; beware of cosine similarity.
  • Usage versus geometry (Fig. 11 B). $r=0.28$, $R^2=0.08$; $\tr[\mZ\T\mZ\,\mD\mD\T]=\norm{\mZ\mD}_F^2\ge0$ explains why some positive relation is unavoidable, but not the Pearson coefficient.
  • PCA maps (Figs. 12, 13, 22). A linear map takes a segment to a segment or a point, so it adds no curvature; it can still hide differences. A shared basis aligns five snow leopards.
  • Position (Figs. 14, 15, 27). Decoding $\approx1.0\to0.23$ over layers; stable rank $9.0\to2.7$; $R^2$ of top-2 $0.63\to1.0$ (inconsistent with the stable rank); $15$ per-image components hold $81\%$; position sits in components 3 and 5; smoothness survives position removal.
  • Interpolative geometry. A conjecture based on prototype heads and KoLeo; the bridge to page 5.

Reflexes

  • A dictionary spectrum → compare with $\sqrt{c/d}$ and the Marchenko–Pastur band, not with a flat line at $1$.
  • A "coherence" claim → compare with the Welch bound ($0.0356$) and with the best known frame ($0.0659$), not with $0$.
  • Two opposite atoms → a signed feature under non-negative codes; use $\abs{\cos}$ if the sign is not the point.
  • A correlation between $\mZ\T\mZ$ and $\mD\mD\T$ → ask whether the diagonal is in; the trace identity makes a positive uncentred inner product automatic.
  • A smooth PCA map → it shows the top-3 coordinates are smooth, and that projection cannot add curvature; it does not show connectedness in $\R^{768}$.
  • "Rank collapses to 2" → on which matrix? Check $\mathrm{srank}\le2/R^2$.
  • "Mixtures of prototypes" → in which space, with what weights?

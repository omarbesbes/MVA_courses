9. Errata: slips and inconsistencies in the paper

Thirteen items, none of which changes the paper's main findings; they matter for a careful reading of Part III and of the figures. Stated factually, with the evidence.

References are to the arXiv v3 PDF and its figures. The first ten items were checked against the source and the figures; items 11–14 are from our reading of the appendices and are worth re-checking against the cited place before being quoted.

#WhereWhatEvidence
1§4, Fig. 7 right, App. Fig. 21 rightThe text mentions one cls-only concept; the bar chart reads about 32.Read the bar “CLS only” in the two figures.
2§7, Def. 2The text refers to a “criterion (iii)”, but the definition has two items, (i) and (ii). Same in v1 and v2.Count the items of Def. 2.
3Fig. 14 (top)The stable ranks (2.6–2.8 in the last layers) are incompatible with the $R^2$ of the top-2 PCs next to them (0.99–1.00). The layer axes also differ (0–11 and 1–12).Bound below. The two panels are not computed on the same object (e.g. uncentred and centred).
4Lemma 1Writes $\Ima(\mK\T)$; with keys as rows of $\mK\in\R^{m\times d_k}$ the logits are $\mK\vq$ and the image is the column space of $\mK$. Softmax is declared $\R^d\to\R^d$ but used on $\R^m$; the proof writes “$\vy=\val\vv$” and “$\vx+\lambda\mathbf 1$”.Dimensions of $\mK\vq$. Corrected on page 5.
5Lemma 1, proofSoftmax never outputs a boundary point: the attainable set is the relative interior of $\conv(\mV)$, whose closure is $\conv(\mV)$.Softmax weights are strictly positive.
6App. G.3“Many norms become affine once the mean and variance are held fixed”: true for batch normalisation, not for LayerNorm, whose statistics are per token and computed at inference. RMSNorm is mentioned; DINOv2 does not use it.The final normalisation of DINOv2 recomputed from the pre-norm tokens (r1, error $2\times10^{-6}$); it depends on each token's own $\mu$ and $\sigma$.
7Prop. 2 / App. Prop. 4, proof“Fix any sublinear $s$ with $0\le s\le h_\X$; then $h_\X=(h_\X-s)+s$” needs $h_\X-s$ sublinear, which fails in general.Numerical counter-example 0.45, 0.45, 1.0 (section 6).
8AA footnote; §7The footnote uses $\mZ$ for archetypes and $\mA$ for coefficients, the main text $\mZ$ for codes and $\mA$ for activations; $\mX$ is the image batch, the activation space and the data matrix.Compare the notations.
9§6, App. FThe position matrix is $\mP\in\R^{256\times d}$ in §6; $N$ and $n$ are used inconsistently in App. F.Compare the two places.
10Fig. 17 leftThe $y$ axis, “distance to nearest sample”, reaches about 13 while the text says cosine distance (maximum 2): the plotted distance is Euclidean.Axis range against the text.
11App. E, random baselineThe baseline is normalised to half the stated Frobenius norm.$\tilde{\mathbf R}=\frac{(\mathbf R+\mathbf R\T)/2}{\|\mathbf R+\mathbf R\T\|_F}\|\mZ\T\mZ\|_F$; since $\|\mathbf R+\mathbf R\T\|_F=2\|(\mathbf R+\mathbf R\T)/2\|_F$, $\|\tilde{\mathbf R}\|_F=\tfrac12\|\mZ\T\mZ\|_F$.
12§6 footnote on the DINO and iBOT headsIt states 128k prototypes, an MLP $4096\to4096\to256$ and two distinct heads. The default configuration of the official release has 65,536 prototypes, hidden width 2048 and a head shared by the two losses.The footnote against the official configuration files (to re-check before quoting; it bears on the interpolation argument of §6, not on Part III).
13App. C.3 and Fig. 6They disagree on which perturbation the shadow family is most sensitive to: the text says median filtering; the profiles of the figure give blur for the shadow family and median filtering for local frequency transitions.App. C.3 text against the sensitivity profiles of Fig. 6 / App. Fig. 18 (to re-check against the figure).

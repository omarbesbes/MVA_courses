7. Summary

What to remember
  1. Definition. Archetypes $\Ac$ ($c\gg d$) in $m$ tiles $\Tc_i$; tile polytopes $\Pc_i=\conv(\Ac_{\Tc_i})$; $\X=\bigmsum_i\Pc_i$; an activation is $\vx=\sum_{i\in S}\vz_i\Ac_{\Tc_i}$ with each $\vz_i$ in a simplex and $|S|\ll m$. Archetypal analysis is the one-tile case. The sparse bounded linear code is the case of segment tiles at the origin.
  2. Attention. One head: $\val\mV\in\conv(\mV)$, weights = codes; reachability needs $\rank\mK+1\ge m$ and fills the relative interior. Affine maps keep the codes (bias once). Multi-head: concatenate-then-project is $\sum_h\vy_h\mW_O^{(h)}$, so the output is in $\bigmsum_h\mW_O^{(h)}\conv(\mV_h)$. Low temperature: vertex, with error $\le\diam(\mV)\sum_{j\ne j^\star}e^{-\Delta_j/\tau}$; support restriction: sub-polytope, face if $S=\argmax\ps{\vw}{\vv_j}$.
  3. Evidence (Fig. 17). Linear chords peak at about 13 from the data, graph paths stay at 4–5; per-image AA beats the SAE ($0.227$) from about 10 archetypes ($0.19$ at 10, $0.116$ at 30); $\mZ\T\mZ$ with about 1000 archetypes is block-diagonal. “Compatible evidence, not proof.”
  4. Implications. Concepts are landmarks and regions. Steering: directional proximity is a bell with peak $e^{-d_\perp}$ at $t^\star$, archetypal proximity $e^{-(1-t)\|\va-\vx\|}$ increases to $1$. Non-identifiability: true (trivially by translation, splitting, regrouping; genuinely for the hexagon = 3 segments = $T\oplus(-T)$), but the paper's proof is flawed ($h_\X-s$ need not be sublinear), and a sum of segments does determine its generators' directions and total lengths.
  5. Limits. Working hypothesis, observational, DINOv2-B only, “we refrain from stating that our results contradict LRH”.

Reflexes

  • When you see “softmax output” $\to$ barycentric coordinates of the values; strictly positive, so relative interior; low temperature $\to$ vertex.
  • When you see “affine map of a convex combination” $\to$ same weights, new points; with several blocks, the bias counts once.
  • When you see “concatenate then project” $\to$ sum of per-head projections $\to$ Minkowski sum of per-head polytopes.
  • When you see “$\X=\bigmsum\Pc_i$ and $|S|\ll m$” $\to$ ask whether it is the full sum (convex) or the union over active sets.
  • When you see “decompose a Minkowski sum” $\to$ pass to support functions; remember that a summand needs $h_\X-s$ sublinear; edges of polygons merge by direction.
  • When you see “steering saturates” $\to$ it is the distance to a landmark, $e^{-\|\vx_t-\va\|}$, not a measurement.
  • When you see “evidence for MRH” $\to$ say what else produces the same picture; the authors do.

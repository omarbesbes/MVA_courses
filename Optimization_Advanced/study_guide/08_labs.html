Labs · TP 1 to TP 4 · five subjects

The labs, worked theoretically

Each lab is a lecture in disguise: denoising is one prox, NMR is Forward–Backward and Douglas–Rachford on an ill-posed kernel, selectivity estimation is a primal-dual algorithm on a simplex, tomography is Majorization–Minimization at scale. Every question of every subject is derived here, with the algorithm, the implementation choices and the behaviour you should expect, so that your notebook explains why and not only what.

  1. Read the subject question, then the "theory it tests". Open the lecture card from the chips before reading the derivation; the lab is an application, never a new result.
  2. Redo each derivation on paper (existence, prox, step size) before opening it: these are exactly the sentences the report must contain.
  3. Code from the algorithm box, not from the sketch: the sketches show the vectorized shape of one step, the box tells you what to iterate and when to stop.
